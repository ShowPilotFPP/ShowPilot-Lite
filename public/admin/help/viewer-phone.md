# Viewer Page › Listen-on-Phone

ShowPilot-Lite doesn't play audio on viewers' phones. Your show's sound reaches people some other way, such as an FM transmitter. The tab keeps its name from the full version of ShowPilot, but in Lite only part of it does anything: the **decoration** settings, which style the now-playing bar on your viewer page.

## The now-playing bar

While a song is playing, a bar slides up at the bottom of your viewer page with the song's cover art, title and artist. Long titles scroll so they can be read in full. It's display only: there's no play button and no sound.

The bar slides away between songs, and stays hidden while the viewer mode is **Off**.

## Listen-on-Phone Player Decoration

These settings change how the now-playing bar looks. They save as soon as you change them, and open viewer pages pick them up within a few seconds while a song is playing. They're separate from the admin's own theme: viewers see what you pick here.

### Decoration theme

Dresses up the bar with seasonal colors and decorations. Choose **None** (the default) or one of: New Year's, Lunar New Year, Valentine's, Mardi Gras, St. Patrick's, Easter, Independence Day, Halloween, Día de los Muertos, Diwali, Thanksgiving, Hanukkah, Christmas, Kwanzaa, or **Snow (winter generic)**.

> **Tip:** Pair the bar's theme with a matching [page effect](help:viewer-effects) for a consistent seasonal look.

### Custom player bar color

Only shown, and only used, when **Decoration theme** is **None**. Handy when you have a themed viewer page and want the bar to match it.

- **Solid color**: pick a color, or type one (for example `#1a1a2e`).
- **Gradient**: pick a **Top** and **Bottom** color and a **Direction** (top to bottom, bottom to top, left to right, right to left, or one of two diagonals).

The **Preview** shows roughly how the bar will look. **Reset to default** goes back to the standard dark bar.

### Enable animations (twinkle, fall, drift)

Lets the decorations move. On by default. Turn it off if you want them to stay still.

> **Tip:** The [song progress bar](help:viewer-effects#song-progress-bar) can sit on the top edge of this bar and take its theme color.

## Listen-on-Phone Launcher Button

This card has no effect in ShowPilot-Lite. There's no Listen button on the viewer page, and the choices here (icon, custom image, round background and size) aren't kept: they may look changed, but they're back to how they were the next time you open the admin. You can ignore this card.

If a template you bring in from the full version of ShowPilot uses the `{LISTEN_BUTTON}` placeholder, it simply shows nothing in Lite, so the page still looks right. See [Viewer page templates](help:templates).
